Selection protocol

Curation over aggregation.

Every indexed piece should help a technical reader understand a mechanism, method, system behavior, or research lesson.

  1. 01

    Technical contribution

    Teaches more than the announcement or abstract alone.

  2. 02

    Specificity

    Provides enough evidence and detail to support technical judgment.

  3. 03

    Durable value

    Remains useful after the immediate release cycle has passed.
