About BlogrXiv

A curated, source-linked index of technical AI research writing, maintained by OpenEnvision.

What BlogrXiv Is

AI research increasingly circulates through writing that sits between formal publication and informal commentary: lab essays that explain system design decisions, independent methodological notes, conference blog-track articles, and engineering write-ups that document failure modes and evaluation protocols papers rarely include. This layer is valuable but hard to search, compare, and revisit.

BlogrXiv preserves and organizes that layer. It is an editorially curated index: every entry links to its canonical source, carries author and lab attribution, and is placed in an eleven-shelf research taxonomy with searchable topic tags. The index complements papers, benchmarks, and bibliographic databases; it does not replace them.

BlogrXiv is not:

  • A paper mirror — entries must add independent narrative beyond the paper itself.
  • A news feed — release announcements without analysis are not indexed.
  • A leaderboard or a ranking of authors, labs, or institutions.
  • A general-purpose blog directory — inclusion follows a written editorial standard, not breadth.
  • A publishing platform — the canonical version of every post lives at its original source.

How Entries Reach the Index

Two intake paths feed one review process. Nothing is published directly from an intake form.

Recommended links

Anyone may recommend an existing public post — including their own — through a GitHub Discussion or by email. Editors verify the source, evaluate the technical contribution, and convert accepted recommendations into index metadata that points at the canonical URL.

Original writing

Authors may submit unpublished Markdown writing through a pull request or by email, with an explicit permission statement. Editors review structure, references, and technical value, request edits where needed, and index accepted pieces with full attribution.

Both paths share one state model: submissions are pending by default, decisions are recorded publicly on GitHub, and only accepted work enters the index. See the editorial guidelines for review criteria and exclusion rules.

A Note from the Founder

I started BlogrXiv with a simple observation: some of the most useful technical writing in AI research never enters the formal record. Careful explanations of mechanisms, honest accounts of failed experiments, and hard-won engineering lessons scatter across personal sites and lab blogs, then disappear from view. An index that treats this writing seriously — with attribution, taxonomy, and an explicit editorial standard — makes the field easier to learn and harder to forget.

Juanxi Tian

Founder, OpenEnvision

What BlogrXiv is

BlogrXiv is a curated discovery system for AI research blogs and technical essays; an auditable, source-linked index; and a taxonomy for navigating that writing by research concept rather than recency.

It is not a paper mirror, a news feed, a leaderboard, a ranking of labs, or a general-purpose blog directory. Recommended links and original writing are pending until an editor accepts them and commits static metadata.

Editorial standard

  • The post teaches a mechanism, method, system behavior, evaluation, or research lesson.
  • It is specific enough to support technical judgment, not generic commentary.
  • Author, lab, or venue is identifiable; the canonical URL is preserved.
  • The piece remains useful after the announcement cycle. Product landings and lab homepages are excluded.
  • It can be placed in one of eleven categories without forcing a neighbor shelf.

Summaries are editorial. They do not replace the original article or reproduce substantial copyrighted text. See Community Guidelines and Submit.
